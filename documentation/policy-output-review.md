# Policy Output Review

## Expected Coverage by Policy Type

Different policy types have different expected coverage levels for each framework. This is intentional and reflects the primary focus of each policy type.

### Privacy Policy
- **GDPR**: ≥70% (Primary focus - privacy policies must comprehensively address GDPR requirements)
- **AI Act**: ≥40% (Secondary - privacy policies may reference AI Act for AI-related data processing)
- **ISO 42001**: ≥60% (Important - privacy policies should reference ISO 42001 for data handling and privacy management)

**Rationale**: Privacy policies are primarily focused on data protection and privacy rights under GDPR. AI Act and ISO 42001 coverage is expected but secondary, as these policies address broader privacy concerns beyond just AI systems.

### AI Usage Policy
- **GDPR**: ≥30% (Secondary - AI usage policies focus on AI Act compliance, GDPR is relevant for data handling)
- **AI Act**: ≥70% (Primary focus - AI usage policies must comprehensively address AI Act requirements)
- **ISO 42001**: ≥70% (Primary focus - AI usage policies should comprehensively address ISO 42001 for AI system management)

**Rationale**: AI Usage policies are primarily focused on AI Act compliance and ISO 42001 management systems. GDPR coverage is expected but lower, as these policies address AI-specific requirements rather than general data protection.

### AI Governance Policy
- **GDPR**: ≥20% (Minimal - AI governance policies focus on governance structures, GDPR is minimal)
- **AI Act**: ≥70% (Primary focus - AI governance policies must comprehensively address AI Act governance requirements)
- **ISO 42001**: ≥70% (Primary focus - AI governance policies should comprehensively address ISO 42001 governance requirements)

**Rationale**: AI Governance policies are primarily focused on organizational governance, oversight, and compliance structures for AI systems. GDPR coverage is intentionally low, as these policies address governance rather than data protection specifics.

### Coverage Threshold Indicators

The PolicyCop scoring system uses color coding based on policy-specific thresholds:
- **Green**: Coverage meets or exceeds the policy-specific threshold
- **Yellow**: Coverage is within 80% of the threshold (acceptable but below target)
- **Red**: Coverage is below 80% of the threshold (needs improvement)

These thresholds are displayed in the PolicyCop score UI with visual indicators showing the target threshold for each framework.
