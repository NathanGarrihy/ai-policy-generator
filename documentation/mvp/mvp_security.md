## 1) Purpose & Principles

1.1 **Mission first.** This agreement exists to protect the company’s long-term success, the founders’ relationship, and investor readiness.  
1.2 **Equality with accountability.** Founders start equal and remain equal while both perform agreed responsibilities. Divergence triggers review and buy-out mechanisms.  
1.3 **No surprises.** Expectations, progress, and decisions are documented. Disagreements are addressed quickly and professionally.

---

## 2) Roles & Domains (what “good” looks like)

2.1 **Founder A (Product/Tech):** Ships the MVP, features, integrations, reliability, and security baselines; maintains prompts/policy logic; supports demos and grant technicals.  
2.2 **Founder B (Market/Growth):** Validates customer need; builds pipeline (leads, pilots, partners); runs grant sourcing to submission; drives public visibility and feedback loops.  
2.3 **Shared:** Strategy, roadmap, incorporation, finance hygiene, security/ethics posture, pricing.  
2.4 **Quarterly performance metrics (high-level):**

**Product/Tech (A):**

- Releases/iterations completed
- TTFG targets
- Export success rate
- Test coverage/QA cadence
- Demo reliability

**Market/Growth (B):**

- Verified leads & meetings
- Pilots/payers signed or advanced
- Grant milestones (eligibility → submission → presentation)
- Content/PR cadence with engagement

## 3) Ownership & Economics

3.1 **Initial equity:** 50% Founder A, 50% Founder B.  
3.2 **No automatic reallocation:** Equity does not move by formula or “vesting.” Accountability enforced via Review → Cure → Buy-Out (Section 6).  
3.3 **No royalties:** The company will not pay perpetual revenue royalties to founders.  
3.4 **Optional success fee (on exit):**  
If agreed: On sale/merger, each founder receives a one-time Success Fee of [3–5]% of net proceeds before pro-rata distribution.  
If not agreed, strike this clause.

---

## 4) IP & Confidentiality

4.1 **Pre-incorporation IP:** Owned by the creating founder.  
4.2 **Exclusive licence at Day 1:** Each founder grants the company an exclusive, royalty-free licence for company purposes.  
4.3 **Assignment trigger:** On pre-seed round, first paying customers, or mutual written agreement — assign IP to company for nominal consideration (€1) or agreed amount.  
4.4 **Company-era IP:** Owned by the company.  
4.5 **Confidentiality:** Use password manager and MFA; avoid disclosure without consent.  
4.6 **Non-compete:** No competing projects for 12 months post-exit within EU/UK in same niche.

---

## 5) Decision-Making & Documentation

5.1 **Operational autonomy:** Each founder has day-to-day authority within their domain.  
5.2 **Major decisions (mutual consent):** Equity issuances, financings, senior hiring/firing, budgets > €[X], pivots, IP assignment, M&A, debt, liquidation.  
5.3 **Weekly sync:** 30–60 minutes for review, blockers, and near-term decisions.  
5.4 **Decision Log:** Records major choices, KPI picks, and outcomes in shared drive/Notion.

---

## 6) Performance Review, Cure & Buy-Out

6.1 **Quarterly review:** Within 10 days of quarter-end, founders review KPIs and mark _Met / Partially Met / Not Met_ with evidence.  
6.2 **Trigger events:**

- (a) Two consecutive quarters of major KPI misses
- (b) 6+ weeks material non-performance
- (c) Serious conduct issue  
  6.3 **Cure period:** 30 days with written Performance Notice and specific KPIs.  
  6.4 **If cured:** Continue.  
  6.5 **If not cured:** Performing founder may:
- (i) Rebalance roles for 90 days
- (ii) Initiate Buy-Out (Section 7)  
  6.6 **Interim freeze:** During buy-out, only ordinary-course actions allowed; record decisions.

---

## 7) Valuation & Buy-Out Mechanics

7.1 **Fair Value:** Independent valuation by qualified Irish accountant or advisor. If disagreement on expert, each picks one; the two choose a third.  
7.2 **Reference methods:** Revenue multiple, DCF, comparable transactions, or cost-to-rebuild plus IP/traction premium.  
7.3 **Payment terms:** 10–20% cash; balance as secured vendor note (12–24 months, 4–6% simple interest). Early repayment allowed.  
7.4 **Right of first refusal:** 45-day match right for non-selling founder.  
7.5 **Good-faith cooperation:** Selling founder signs required documents and provides up to 30 days of transitional assistance.

---

## 8) Deadlock Resolution

8.1 **Mediation:** If a major decision deadlocks for 30 days, attempt mediation.  
8.2 **Shotgun clause:** After 60 days unresolved, either founder may offer a buy/sell price per share. Recipient must sell or buy on same terms within 45 days (vendor-note terms apply).  
8.3 **Goal:** Prevent paralysis, maintain fairness.

---

## 9) Capital Calls & Expenses

9.1 **Pre-approved spend:** Up to €150 per item, €500/month combined pre-revenue.  
9.2 **Capital Calls (50/50):** Larger spends require mutual written approval, split 50/50, payable within 10 business days.  
9.3 **If one fronts payment:** After 30 days, converts to Founder Loan:

- Principal: fronted amount
- Interest: 4% simple p.a.
- Priority: repaid from first €15k net operating cash flow before dividends
- No equity impact unless agreed in writing  
  9.4 **Reimbursements:** Within 10 business days upon receipt upload.

---

## 10) Non-Solicit, Ethics & Security

10.1 **Non-solicit:** 12 months post-exit, no soliciting staff, contractors, or customers.  
10.2 **Security & ethics:** Use MFA, vaults, anonymized data, follow security checklist.  
10.3 **Reputation:** No public statements materially harming company or co-founder.
